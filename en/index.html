Qarjym Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Contact: tastaq.support@gmail.com

In short

Qarjym does not collect your data. We have no server and no accounts. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted.

What is stored on your phone

Accounts, operations, debts, plans and settings. The database is encrypted; the key is kept in the system secure storage (Keychain / Keystore). We cannot see or access it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no crash reports sent to us.
  • No sign-up and no cloud sync in this version.
  • We do not sell or share data.

Internet requests

To show a total across currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates. It makes plain GET requests to three public sources:

  • open.er-api.com
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net (the fawazahmed0/currency-api package)
  • latest.currency-api.pages.dev

These requests are the same for everyone and contain none of your personal or financial data. No account, identifier or entered amount is sent. Each of these services may see your IP address and the time of the request, under its own policy.

Notifications and files

Reminders are local notifications created on your phone. If you import a bank statement (PDF), it is read and processed on your phone only and is not uploaded. Home-screen widgets show data from the app on your device only; names of debts are hidden on widgets by default, and you can turn this off in Settings. When you export or share data, you choose where it goes.

Your control

Delete the app to delete its data. Back up exports yourself if you need them.

Children

Qarjym is not aimed at children under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions: tastaq.support@gmail.com.

Qarjym gives general information and is not financial, tax or legal advice.
